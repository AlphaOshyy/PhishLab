You interacted with a fictional sign-in simulation. Real phishing pages often copy familiar layouts to make a request feel trustworthy.

Before entering credentials:
  • Check the complete website domain in the address bar.
  • Open the service using its official app or a saved bookmark.
  • Be suspicious of urgent links sent by email, text, or direct message.
